# Privacy Policy

Tint for Notion does not collect, transmit or sell any data. It has no analytics, no tracking, no accounts and no servers, and it never reads the content of your Notion pages.

## What is stored

The extension stores only your settings:

- **Your theme choice and whether the extension is on.** These are kept in your browser's extension storage (`chrome.storage.sync`). If you have browser sync turned on, your browser may sync them across your own devices through your browser account. The developer has no access to them.
- **A copy of the current theme's colors.** This is kept in the browser's local storage for each Notion site you visit, so the theme appears before the page paints. It contains only color values and is removed when you turn the extension off.

## Permissions

- **`storage`:** saves the settings above.
- **Access to `app.notion.com`, `www.notion.so` and `*.notion.site`:** lets the extension apply the theme on Notion pages. It runs on no other sites.
